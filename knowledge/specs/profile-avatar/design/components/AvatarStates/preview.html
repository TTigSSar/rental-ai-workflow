Rules

Uploading: scrim + white spinner over the old face, badge at 45%, button aria-disabled, rest of the page inert. A role=status caption says “Uploading photo…” so screen readers hear it. The new photo replaces the face when saved; no preview, no crop. Error: the old face stays, the panel sits under the avatar with an icon and words (role=alert).
